# Generic Sales Script — Local Service Business Outreach

**Use:** Cold call / first discovery call for local service businesses
**Tools referenced:** GoHighLevel, Zapier/n8n/Make, webhooks/APIs, HTML/CSS funnels
**Tone:** Practical, conversational, no fluff

## 1. Opening — Get Permission 15-30 sec

Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] from [Your Agency]. We help [niche, e.g., home service / auto / clinic] businesses around [City/Area] get more booked appointments without increasing ad spend.

I know you're busy — is this a bad time, or can I take 30 seconds to share why I'm calling?

*If yes:* Great, thanks.
*If no:* No problem. Best time to call back — morning or afternoon [day]?

## 2. Hook — Surface Pain 30-60 sec

We've been working with a few [niche] owners who were frustrated with leads coming in but not turning into booked jobs. Usually it's slow follow-up, leads sitting in Facebook Messenger/Forms/Calls, and no clear handoff to the calendar.

Does that sound familiar at all, or is your lead-to-appointment flow dialed in right now?

Listen and reflect back the top pain in their words.

## 3. Value Snapshot 60-120 sec

Got it. The way we solve that is pretty simple:

1. **Capture everything in one place.** We connect your forms, ads, calls, and Messenger into GoHighLevel so no lead gets lost.
2. **Automate follow-up.** Instant SMS + email via Zapier / n8n / Make, plus call tasks for your team. We keep following up for 30 days, not 24 hours.
3. **Make it easy to book.** Simple funnel pages and calendar links that pre-qualify and book directly, built with HTML/CSS.

Most clients see more booked calls within 2-3 weeks without hiring extra staff.

Is increasing booked appointments without more ad spend a priority for you right now?

## 4. Qualify 2-3 questions

* Where do most of your leads come from today?
* How many qualified calls are you booking per month right now?
* Who handles follow-up, and how fast do you usually reply?

If they are a fit, move to next step. If not, give a quick tip and leave door open.

## 5. Next Step — Low Friction CTA

I don't want to pitch now. If it makes sense, I can do a quick 15-minute audit of your current lead flow — where leads drop, how fast you follow up, and where revenue is likely leaking. No cost, no sales pressure.

Would Tuesday or Thursday work better for a brief look?

## Objection Handling

**'I already have marketing.'**
Totally fair. We usually find gaps in follow-up speed and handoffs, even with good marketing. Happy to show you where, even if you stay with them.

**'We're too busy.'**
That's exactly when this hurts most. A 15-min audit shows you the leaks; you can decide later.

**'Send me info.'**
Sure, what's the best email? I'll send a 1-page snapshot of the audit. Can I follow up Thursday to see what you thought?

**'Not interested.'**
No worries. Quick tip: most local businesses lose 60-70% of leads to slow follow-up. If that ever changes, I'm here.

## Close

Thanks again [First Name]. I'll send a calendar invite for [day/time]. If anything changes, just reply to the email. Have a great day.
