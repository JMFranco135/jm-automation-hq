1.
Purpose
This SOP gives the setter one universal script to run the qualifier call for any new client. It exists so every inbound lead gets the same 2-minute call: confirm the opt-in, surface the real reason they reached out, and book the appointment - whether that appointment is a virtual consultation or an in-person visit. Read it, fill in the client-specific blanks, and run every call from it.

2.
Role Overview
The setter runs the first touch with a new lead after they opt in from a Facebook or Instagram ad. The job is not to sell the service on this call; it is to qualify the lead and lock the appointment on the calendar. One call, one outcome: a booked, confirmed appointment the lead has added to their own calendar.

2.1 Core Responsibilities
Call every new lead while it is fresh and confirm what they opted in for.
Run discovery to find the real, personal reason they reached out.
Book the appointment and get the lead to confirm it on their own calendar.
Point them to the pre-appointment video so they show up warm.
Keep the call to roughly 2 minutes - this is a qualifier, not a pitch.
3.
Guiding Principles
When the steps don't cover the situation, these decide it.

It is a 2-minute qualifier, not a pitch
You are checking fit and booking the appointment, nothing more. The selling happens at the appointment, not here.
Lead with their reason
The personal reason you surface in discovery is the fuel for the booking. Get it before you ask for the time.
Assume the booking
Offer two specific times, never "when are you free." The booking is not done until they have added it to their own calendar.
Adapt, do not improvise: the blanks flex per client, but the structure never changes.

5.
Part 1 - Introduction
What this is: the opener that confirms the opt-in and earns the first small yes. Good outcome: they remember opting in and agree to the 2-minute qualifier.

Hey [NAME]?

Not sure if this sounds familiar or not… you reached out from my Facebook/Instagram about [TIME AGO] about [XYZ]. Does that ring a bell?

Listen, this call is literally 2 minutes, just to ask you a couple questions about [XYZ] to see if we can actually even help you. I guess, what made you reach out?

6.
Part 2 - Discovery
What this is: the type of question to ask - the closer picks the actual questions per client. Good outcome: a juicy, personal reason they want this solved.

Ask questions that dig into the pain point.
Ask questions that bring that pain point to the surface.
Ask questions that make that pain point hit more personally (business → personal).
Timeline questions - how long this has been going on.
What they've already tried to fix it.
Qualify for fit - where they're at right now (e.g. monthly revenue for business offers).
Mirror it back to confirm - "so it sounds like [issue], you've tried [past attempts], and it's causing [pain]… am I getting that right?"
Goal of discovery
By the end you should have a juicy, personal reason they want this solved. That reason is the fuel for the booking.
7.
Part 3 - Booking Process
What this is: position, book, and confirm the appointment. Good outcome: a booked appointment the lead has added to their own calendar, with the video sent.

Based on everything we've talked about, [NAME], I definitely think we can actually help you. (optional positioning: everything we do is customized - what I've seen help people in your position is how we [MECHANISM].)

From here, what we usually do is book a [CONSULTATION NAME], where we [WHAT HAPPENS IN IT]. How does tomorrow at 6, or [day] at [time], work for you? What works best in the next couple of days?

Awesome, [NAME], we're excited to have you. Is [email] a good email for you? (book them in)

You should have received an invitation to the appointment. Do you see it yet? If you can do me a favor and just star that email - okay, good. Now go ahead and go to the bottom of that email. You'll see which calendar you use: Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple. Go ahead and click on your calendar and add it - that confirms the appointment on our end.

Okay, cool. By the way, in that email there's a video that goes very in depth and walks you through exactly how we work. If you watch it before your appointment, it'll make it significantly more productive. Hey, you think you can set aside less than 10 minutes to watch it before? Awesome. It should have just been texted to you, or it'll be in that email.

Any other questions before we hop off? … Okay, great. I'll see you at [time].

8.
Red Flags
Stop and fix
The lead does not remember opting in and stays cold - re-confirm the [XYZ]; if there's still nothing, it may be a bad lead.
You start pitching the service on this call - stop; this call only qualifies and books.
You book without getting the personal reason - the appointment will be weak and more likely to no-show.
The lead says they'll "add it later" - walk them through adding it to their calendar on the call, or it is not confirmed.
You offer "whenever works" instead of two specific times.
