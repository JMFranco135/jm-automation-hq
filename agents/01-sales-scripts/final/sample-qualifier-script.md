## 1. Introduction — Confirm opt-in
Hey [NAME]?

Not sure if this sounds familiar or not… you reached out [TIME AGO] about [XYZ]. Does that ring a bell?

Listen, this call is literally 2 minutes, just to ask you a couple questions about [XYZ] to see if we can actually even help you. I guess, what made you reach out?

*Good outcome:* they remember opting in and agree to 2 minutes.

## 2. Discovery — Surface real reason
Ask:
- What makes you want to solve this now?
- How long has this been going on?
- What have you already tried? What happened?
- Where are you at right now? e.g., monthly revenue, qualified calls, jobs booked
- Why is it important now? Business → personal

Mirror back:
> So it sounds like [issue], you’ve tried [past attempts], and it’s causing [pain]. Am I getting that right?

Goal: juicy personal reason that fuels the booking.

## 3. Booking Process — Position, offer two times, confirm calendar
Based on everything we’ve talked about, [NAME], I definitely think we can actually help you. Everything we do is customized – what I’ve seen help people in your position is how we [MECHANISM].

From here, what we usually do is book a [CONSULTATION NAME], where we [WHAT HAPPENS IN IT]. How does tomorrow at 6, or [DAY] at [TIME], work for you?

Awesome, [NAME], we’re excited to have you. Is [EMAIL] a good email for you?

You should have received an invitation. Do you see it yet? Star that email, then go to the bottom and click your calendar – Google/Outlook/Apple – and add it. That confirms the appointment on our end.

In that email there’s a 10-minute video that walks through exactly how we work. If you watch it before, it’ll make the call more productive. Think you can set aside <10 minutes before?

Any other questions? Great, I’ll see you at [TIME].

## DO-CONFIRM checklist
- Opt-in confirmed; 2-minute frame set
- Pain surfaced, made personal, mirrored & confirmed
- Positioned with [MECHANISM]
- Two specific times offered
- Email captured, invite sent, calendar added on call
- Pre-appointment video mentioned

## Red flags
- Lead doesn’t remember opt-in → re-confirm [XYZ]; if still cold, flag
- Pitching on this call → stop, qualify & book only
- No personal reason → appointment will be weak/no-show
- “Add later” → walk them through adding now
- “When are you free?” → offer two specific times
