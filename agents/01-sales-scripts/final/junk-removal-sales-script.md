## Part 2 — Discovery

*(Pick 4–6. Goal: a juicy, personal reason — the fuel for the booking.)*

**Surface the pain:**
- Which spaces are we talking about — garage, backyard, side yard, the whole house?
- What's the biggest thing sitting out there right now? *(They'll usually name it: old furniture, boxes, broken stuff.)*

**Make it personal (business → personal for a homeowner = "list → headspace"):**
- Sounds like the stuff itself isn't even the worst part — it's everything else on your list that waits behind it. Does that hit?
- When you picture that space cleared, what do you actually see yourself doing with it?

**Timeline:**
- How long has that stuff been sitting there?

**What they've tried:**
- Have you tried tackling it yourself, or rented a dumpster before? What stopped it from getting done?

**Qualify for fit / objections in disguise:**
- Is there anything you'd want handled carefully — stuff inside the house, painted walls, that kind of thing?
- Do you care where the stuff ends up, or is it all just "make it disappear"?

**Mirror it back:**
So it sounds like [issue — e.g., the garage has become the dumping ground], you've tried [past attempts — e.g., a dumpster weekend that never happened], and it's causing [pain — e.g., you can't use the space and it's on your mind constantly]… am I getting that right?

## Objection Handles (built from the avatar)

**"I've got a shop/handyman/neighbor who might do it cheaper."**
Totally makes sense — most of our clients had someone in mind too. Out of curiosity, what's kept you from just getting it done with them so far? *(Surfaces the real blocker: reliability or damage fear. Then:)* That's exactly why we do the free estimate first — you see the crew, how we treat the property, and the exact price before anything happens.

**"What if you scratch the walls / damage anything?"**
Great question — and honestly, that's a fair worry in this industry. The crew that shows up is a small, careful team, and we walk the space with you before we move a thing. If everything looks good after the estimate, you'll have peace of mind. If it doesn't, you decide — no push.

**"Does everything just go to the dump?"**
I'd be wondering the same thing. We keep as much reusable material out of the landfill as we can — usable items get rehomed or recycled, and only true junk gets hauled off. You'll see exactly how we sort it during the walkthrough.

**"I need to think about it / I'm too busy."**
I get it — most people who reach out are slammed, that's kind of the whole problem. The estimate is free and takes the guesswork out: you'll know the exact price and plan, and you decide what happens after. What's a morning or afternoon that works better?

**"How much is it?" (before booking)**
It really depends on volume and what we're hauling — that's exactly why the walkthrough is free, so you get the exact number on the spot instead of a guess over the phone. Fair?
